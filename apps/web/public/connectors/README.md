# Connector icons

One-color brand icons for the connector catalog and for the link pills in messages (`packages/client/src/lib/link-pills.ts`, the reason for Jira's and Google Slides' here): the app draws them as a mask in the brand color (or the ink, for near-black, very light or many-colored brands). Brand colors live with the catalog in code (`CONNECTOR_BRANDS` in `packages/engine/src/connectors/listing.ts`), and every connector in the catalog has an icon (a test checks it).

Most come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.32.0 (CC0 1.0, public domain). `square.svg` is Square's own mark, the first three subpaths (the mark without the wordmark) of `Black/Square_Logo_2025_Black.svg` in the logo set at https://squareup.com/us/en/press/logo (https://assets.ctfassets.net/2d5q1td6cyxq/3xNJXOyxBSmmJ1lUafqHGJ/0d3bcea3bf9426dbf9a54f424e3d1fab/Square_Logo.zip), unchanged and scaled to the 24×24 view box. Square shows it only in black or white, which the ink gives in each theme. The others Simple Icons doesn't have are the brand's own mark, from its site or press kit, reduced to one color and fitted like a Simple Icons icon (the drawing itself, not the canvas it came on, spans the 24×24 view box on its long side and is centred on the short one): AbacatePay, Ahrefs, Amplitude, Attio, Close, Fireflies, Gamma, Granola, Himetrica, Jam, Jotform, Klaviyo, MillionSend, monday.com, Superwall and Tally. Five more are the mark each vendor serves itself, its paths unchanged and fitted the same way, without any tile or wordmark around it (fetched 2026-11-15): AgentMail's is the five paths before the wordmark in the logo drawn inline on https://www.agentmail.to; AgentPhone's is https://agentphone.ai/landing/brand/logomark.svg, both of its paths; Vapi's is the mark in https://vapi.ai/icon.svg, without its dark rounded square; Whop's is the path of the `whop-logo-icon` component whop.com loads (https://whop.com/_web/assets/gha-43693072263-1/whop-logo-icon-D0PbUtOU.js); You.com's is https://you.com/icon.svg. Two more were fetched the same way on 2026-11-17: Agentcard's is the mark of https://agentcard.com/icon.svg, its three paths; Telnyx's is its whole logo, the six paths of the wordmark in telnyx.com's own header (https://dila2qpfw3s64.cloudfront.net/_next/static/media/telnyx-logo-text.3ol7qfk480jrf.svg), because Telnyx publishes its mark alone only as a PNG and its media kit asks that the horizontal logo not be cropped (https://telnyx.com/media-kit). LinkedIn's is the "in" mark of the logo in linkedin.com's own header, https://static.licdn.com/aero-v1/sc/h/8fkga714vy9b2wk5auqo5reeb, its path unchanged and scaled from 21 to 24.

`amazons3.svg` is drawn for this repository (a bucket, the shape the service is known by) rather than taken from Amazon, whose marks Simple Icons no longer carries; `backblaze.svg` is Simple Icons' 16.32.0 icon. Both name a place a workspace can keep its files (Settings › Storage, `apps/web/src/components/StorageSettings.tsx`), with their colors there.

A mark in its maker's colors, which a mask would flatten (PayPal's, Canva's, Outlook's), is in `../brands/`. The mailboxes' marks (Gmail, Outlook, iCloud) and colors live in `MAIL_BRANDS` in `packages/client/src/lib/mail.ts`.

Each icon is a trademark of its owner, used only to name the service.
