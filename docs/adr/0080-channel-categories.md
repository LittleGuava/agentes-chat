# 0080. Channel categories in the sidebar

- Status: Accepted (built: the server, the shared client, the web and the phone app). Amended 2026-10-30: §6 and §7 (channels take an order in a workspace with no categories too), and §6 again (a category with no channel someone can see doesn't show for them, unless they manage channels). Amended 2026-11-16: §1, §6 and §7 ("Usar a minha ordem", a person's own order of the sidebar's rows, off unless they turn it on)
- Date: 2026-10-30
- Code: `packages/engine/src/channel-categories.ts`, `packages/engine/src/channels.ts` (`createChannel`, `listChannels`), `packages/db/src/schema/chat.ts` (`channel_categories`, `channels.category_id`, `channels.position`), `apps/server/src/routes/app/channels.ts`, `apps/web/src/components/Sidebar.tsx`, `apps/web/src/components/ChannelCategoryDialogs.tsx`, `apps/mobile/src/features/categories/*`, `apps/mobile/src/features/chats/logic.ts` (`homeLayout`); a person's own order: `packages/engine/src/sidebar-items.ts` (`myOwnOrder`, `setMyOwnOrder`), `packages/client/src/lib/own-order.ts`, `apps/web/src/hooks/useSidebarOwnOrder.ts`, `apps/web/src/hooks/useRowOrderDrag.ts`, `apps/web/src/components/SidebarSetup.tsx`, `apps/mobile/src/features/categories/menus.tsx`, `apps/mobile/src/features/me/PreferencePages.tsx` (`OwnOrderPage`)

## Context

With a dozen channels, one alphabetical "Canais" list mixes every team's channels. People asked to group them: named sections in the sidebar that open and close.

## Decision

1. **The organization's layout.** A category belongs to the organization (`channel_categories`, with `org_id`) and everyone sees the same sidebar. Its name is kept as typed, at most 40 characters, unique per organization ignoring case (the engine checks the case; the table's unique key is the name as typed). Opening and closing a category is each person's own, in their browser, like the sidebar's other sections.
2. **Who arranges it.** Admins (`configure`) create, rename, reorder and delete categories. Whoever may change a channel (its creator or an admin, as for renaming it) puts it in a category or moves it. Whoever creates a channel may put it in an existing category as they create it.
3. **Order.** `channel_categories.position` orders the categories; `channels.position` orders the channels inside theirs, then by name. Channels with no category (`category_id` null) come first, with no header. Moves renumber the whole category in one transaction (`placeChannel`, `placeChannelCategory`). A group nobody arranged by hand has every position at 0 and reads by name, so a new channel joins it at 0 and the list stays alphabetical, as before categories; once arranged, a new channel goes to its end.
4. **Deleting a category** moves its channels to the end of the ones with none, in their order. Archiving a channel leaves its place as it is.
5. **Live.** Every change sends an org broadcast `channels.layout` with no names in it (private channels stay private); clients refetch the channel list and the categories.
6. **The sidebar.** A category is a section like Agentes: same header, chevron and a + to create a channel in it. Closed, it still shows the open channel and the unread ones. Channels move from their right-click menu ("Mover para…"), the channel details (Categoria) or by dragging. *Amended 2026-10-30:* dragging works with no categories too. In a workspace that has none, the channels drag above or below each other inside "Canais", for the same people, and land through the same `placeChannel` (category null). Before, only a workspace with categories could drag, and with none the list stayed by name. *Amended 2026-10-30 (what each person sees):* someone who doesn't manage channels sees a category only while it holds a channel they can see (the public ones and the private ones they are in, as `listChannels` gives them). A category of private channels they're not in no longer shows them an empty header, and with no category left their channels show under "Canais", as in a workspace with none. Who manages channels (`manage_channels`, ADR 0085) sees every category, empty ones too, to drop channels in. The places that pick a category ("Mover para…", the channel details, Criar canal) still list all of them. The phone's Conversas already leaves out a section with no rows. *Amended 2026-10-31 (the web's look):* a category no longer shares Agentes' header. Lines set the sidebar's parts apart (the tools, the channels, the agents, the people), and Agentes, Mensagens diretas and Canais (with no categories) are titled in small capitals with their chevron after the title, so none reads as a category. A category keeps its header, and its channels line up with the ones in none: every row's mark (an icon, a channel's "#", a face, a category's chevron) sits in one column and every name in the next. A channel's "#" has the chevron's size and ink.
7. **The phone.** Conversas shows the same layout: Canais (no category), one section per category in order, each its five latest and "Ver todos", a closed category keeping its unread channels. The channel's ⋯ sheet and a long press on its row open "Categoria", a pushed screen to pick one; a long press on a category's header gives admins Rename, Categories and New category, all pushed screens. The order moves one step at a time (Move up, Move down): the app has no drag-to-reorder list. *Amended 2026-10-30:* the channel's "Categoria" screen also has its place in the list ("Lugar na lista"): Move up and Move down among the channels of its category, or the ones with none, and where it sits ("2º de 5."), so the phone orders channels with no categories too. The home still lists each section's channels by latest activity, so that order shows in the web's sidebar.

## Amendment (2026-11-16): "Usar a minha ordem", a person's own order, off unless they turn it on

### What this ADR decided, and why

The sidebar is the organization's layout (§1): admins make and order the categories, whoever may change a channel puts it in its place (§2, §3), and everyone sees the same channels in the same order. The reason is organizational, not a matter of security or privacy: a team that groups its channels wants every member to find them in the same place, and a newcomer to read the workspace the way the people who arranged it meant. Nothing a person can see changes with the order; who sees which channel is `listChannels`' rule and ADR 0085's, untouched here.

### What the option adds

People asked to arrange their own conversations, each in their own order, without changing anyone else's. ADR 0083's amendment of 2026-11-15 gave them that for the pinned rows only. Now each person has a switch, "Usar a minha ordem" (Use my order), in "Personalizar a barra lateral" on the web and in Você on the phone. It is off for everyone until they turn it on. On:

- **Within each section, for that person alone.** The channels with no category, each category's channels, each setor's agents and the people under Mensagens diretas show in the person's order. On the web every row's menu has "Mover para cima" and "Mover para baixo", and the channels and the people also drag inside their own section (`useRowOrderDrag`, which drops a row only on a row of its section). Agents move from their menu only (see the last item under "What stays forbidden"). On the phone a row's long press has the same two, and Conversas and "Ver todos" list each section in that order instead of by latest activity. The pinned rows keep their own order above them (ADR 0083, 2026-11-15).
- **A row nobody placed for them keeps its place in everyone's order.** The ids the person ordered take the places those rows hold in the common order, in the person's order, and any other row stays where the common order puts it (`inOwnOrder`). So a new channel, or one an admin moved into the category, comes in where it would for everyone, and a row that left the section holds no place. A move saves the whole section as the person sees it (`placeInOwnOrder`, `stepInOwnOrder`), so the next new row still lands in its common place.
- **Saved per person, on the server.** `organization_members.sidebar_items` keeps it under `own` (`on` plus the ids per kind, `channels`, `people`, `agents`, at most 500 each), next to the pins and the tools. It is read and written at `GET`/`PUT …/me/sidebar/own-order`. The same order shows on every device. No migration: the column is the person's own jsonb.
- **Off brings the common order back, and keeps the person's.** Turning it off only clears `on`, so turning it on again finds the order as they left it.

### Why off by default keeps this decision

Off, the sidebar is exactly what §1 to §7 say. Every section reads in the workspace's order, no row offers a move of one's own, and the workspace's drag of a channel works as before (the tests that hold this ADR stay as they were). A person who never opens the setting never sees the difference, and a person who turns it on changes only what they see. An admin who arranges the workspace still arranges it for everyone. A member with the option on sees the admin's new categories and channels arrive where the admin put them, among the rows they ordered themselves.

### What stays forbidden

- Changing anyone else's sidebar. The option is read and written only for the person in the session, and no route takes another member's id.
- Changing the workspace's order through it. A personal move never calls `placeChannel` or `placeChannelCategory`, and with the option on a channel's drag on the web is a personal one. So someone who arranges the workspace and turns the option on gives up, while it is on, the web's drag of the workspace's order (inside a category and across categories). "Mover para…" and the channel's details still change a channel's category, and the phone's "Lugar na lista" still moves it in everyone's order. To drag the workspace's order on the web, they turn the option off. One drag can't mean both, as for a pinned row (ADR 0083, 2026-11-15).
- Ordering the categories, or moving a channel to another category, for oneself. Categories and the channels in them are still the organization's (§1, §2). A personal order moves a row only inside the section where the organization put it.
- Seeing more. The option orders the rows the person already sees and nothing else.
- Moving an agent to another setor by its personal order. With the option on an agent moves from its menu only, because the drag of who manages agents already means "to another setor" (ADR 0083 §7).

This amends §1 (each person's own order on top of the organization's layout, only for whoever turns it on), §6 and §7 (the moves of one's own on the web and the phone), and replaces the last consequence of ADR 0083's amendment of 2026-11-15 ("it stays out until a decision changes that").
